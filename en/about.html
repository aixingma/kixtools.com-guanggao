✦ About M3U8Lab

A Streaming Workbench That Fits in a Tab

M3U8Lab bundles HLS playback, testing and conversion into free browser-based tools — open to developers and everyday viewers alike, with nothing to install.

Who We Are

M3U8Lab is a small toolkit focused on one thing: making HLS streams easy to open, inspect and convert. We think streaming tech shouldn't require an SDK download just to check whether a link works.

Everything here runs on capabilities your browser already has. No installers, and no video data ever touches our servers — your streams and your privacy stay where they belong, on your machine.

🎯

Why We Built It

Diagnosing a stream shouldn't take a media server and a stack of docs. Our goal: open a tab, paste a link, and know within seconds whether it plays, converts, or needs deeper digging.

🔐

Local by Design

Playback and transcoding both happen inside your browser via WebAssembly. We never store, forward, or log the content that passes through.

🌍

Five Interfaces, One Toolset

The full toolkit is available in English, Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese and Korean, with the same features behind every language.

Core Features

  • Streaming Playback: open m3u8 / HLS links directly in the browser, adaptive bitrate included.
  • Offline Conversion: turn an m3u8 stream into an MP4 you can keep on your own drive.
  • Iframe Embedding: one snippet puts the player on any third-party site.
  • Every Modern Browser: desktop and mobile, no plugins or extensions needed.
