Answers Before You Ask

What people want to know ahead of — and just after — their first stream

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. M3U8Lab works anonymously: open the page, paste a link, play. Nothing about your session is stored or tied to a profile.

Can I watch live broadcasts here?

Yes. Live HLS playlists load exactly like on-demand ones — the player follows the sliding window and keeps requesting fresh segments as they appear.

Where do I find the m3u8 address for a video?

Open the source page in your browser, start the video, then filter the Network panel of your developer tools for "m3u8". Our step-by-step tutorial covers the exact clicks.

Is there a cap on stream length?

None. Because data is piped directly from the source into your browser, a feature-length film plays just as easily as a ten-second clip.
